3.1 Key 1: Single-Session Invariant Fast Filter (<5ms)

Key 1 evaluates single-session telemetry at Form Step 2 before executing graph calculations:

Click-Dwell Variance Equation
$$\mu_{\text{dwell}} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N d_i, \quad \sigma^2_{\text{dwell}} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N (d_i - \mu_{\text{dwell}})^2$$

Biological Invariant: Real human tendons cannot press and release physical switches with microsecond precision. If $\sigma^2_{\text{dwell}} < 15.0\text{ms}^2$ or instantaneous $d_i \le 2.0\text{ms}$ (programmatic .click()), Key 1 trips: flagged_automation.

Pre-Click Pointer Trajectory Density
$$k_{\text{hover}} = \sum_{t = t_{\text{click}}-500\text{ms}}^{t_{\text{click}}} \mathbf{1}(\text{event} == \text{pointermove})$$

Browser automation frameworks typically dispatch click events directly without prior pointer movements ($k_{\text{hover}} = 0$).
