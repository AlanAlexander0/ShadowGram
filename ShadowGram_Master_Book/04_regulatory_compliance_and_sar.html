CH 4 Regulatory Compliance, Legal SAR Dossier & Safeguards

4.1 Statutory Adverse Action: ECOA & Regulation B

The CFPB withdrew Circular 2023-03 on May 12, 2025 during an administrative cleanup. However, the underlying federal statute did not change.

  • Equal Credit Opportunity Act (15 U.S.C. § 1691): Federal law passed by the United States Congress.
  • Regulation B (12 CFR § 1002.9): Mandates that creditors provide specific, accurate principal reasons when taking adverse action.
  • Relying on an unexplained "88% Risk Score" creates direct regulatory and legal liability.

4.2 Global Directives: EU AI Act & RBI Guidelines

  • EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689): Annex III categorizes credit assessment AI as High-Risk AI Systems. Articles 13 & 14 mandate explainability and human oversight. Penalties reach €35 Million or 7% of global turnover.
  • Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Digital Lending Master Directions: Banks cannot outsource core credit assessment to black-box software and must maintain audit logs for credit denials.

4.4 False Positive Protection (The 3-Tier Safeguard)

  1. 3-Layer Minimum Rule: An edge requires a match on $\ge 3$ layers. An authentic human applying during an attack fails the kinetic jerk and keystroke flight-time checks, keeping their node isolated.
  2. Zero Permanent Bans: ShadowGram never hard-deletes accounts. It issues a Quarantine / Step-Up Challenge.
  3. Friction-Free Escalation: Quarantined users complete a fast verification step (1-rupee UPI penny-drop or Account Aggregator consent). Genuine humans verify in ten seconds; headless bots fail and abandon.
