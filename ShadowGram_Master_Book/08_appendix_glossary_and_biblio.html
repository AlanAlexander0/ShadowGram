Master Glossary (ASD-STE100 Technical Terms)

  • Autonomous AI Swarm: A group of software agents that coordinate actions across multiple accounts without human control.
  • Synthetic Identity: A fabricated identity made by combining authentic government identification numbers with fabricated personal data.
  • Kinetic Jerk: The third mathematical derivative of position with respect to time ($d^3x/dt^3$). Measures the rate of change of acceleration.
  • Louvain Modularity ($Q$): A mathematical algorithm that measures the density of links inside communities compared to links between communities.
  • Denial-of-Wallet (DoW): A cyber-attack that forces an organization to spend financial resources on third-party API verification fees.
  • Why Card: An interface window that displays verifiable factual evidence explaining why accounts were grouped into a cluster.
